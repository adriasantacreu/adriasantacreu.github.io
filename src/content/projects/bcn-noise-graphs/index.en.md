### Motivation
Acoustic pollution is a major environmental health hazard in dense metropolitan areas. This project investigates sound records captured by municipal acoustic stations across Barcelona to provide an intuitive neighborhood diagnostic.

### Technical solution
Data parsing, cleaning, and formatting from the **Open Data BCN** portal integrated into an interactive web dashboard. Ambient decibel metrics (day and night measurements) are mapped across district boundaries to surface longitudinal noise patterns.

### Key features
* **Territorial comparisons**: Granular bar charts and time series broken down by district and street typologies.
* **WHO health benchmarks**: Immediate visual alignment against WHO maximum recommended acoustic exposure thresholds.
* **Client-side rendering**: Fast, standalone browser visualization with minimal bandwidth footprint.

### Impact
Offers citizens and researchers an approachable, transparent tool to evaluate urban noise trends, translating complex decibel registries into actionable visual knowledge.
