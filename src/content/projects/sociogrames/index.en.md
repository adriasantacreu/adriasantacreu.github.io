### Motivation
Understanding interpersonal dynamics within a classroom is essential for fostering healthy school climates, detecting isolation risks early, and assembling balanced collaborative groups. Manual analysis of sociometric tests is tedious and often overlooks subtle patterns.

### Technical solution
Built with **Python**, the application processes peer choice surveys, computes adjacency matrices, and leverages network graph algorithms to project mutual connections, group clusters, and natural leaders.

### Key features
* **Role mapping**: Immediate identification of central peer influencers, bridges, and socially isolated students.
* **Local privacy**: Fully local execution ensuring student information is never dispatched to cloud services.
* **Intuitive network graphs**: Clean, configurable topological maps designed for teachers and pedagogical guidance teams.

### Impact
Empowers educators and counselors with objective, reproducible data to guide classroom seating arrangements, anti-bullying prevention, and cooperative group design.
